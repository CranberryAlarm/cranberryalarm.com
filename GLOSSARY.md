# Cranberry Alarm Website

The public home of Cranberry Alarm, where sponsors, teams and individuals find everything the team has published and see the reach it has had.

## Language

**Cranberry Alarm**:
Central Indiana's Robot in 3 Days team.
_Avoid_: Cranberry Alarm Robotics

**Ri3D**:
Robot in 3 Days: designing and building a robot for the new FRC game in the three days after kickoff, in public.
_Avoid_: RI3D, Robot in Three Days

**Season**:
One year's Ri3D effort, named by the FRC game year (for example 2026).
_Avoid_: Year, build

**Featured Season**:
The newest Season. Before kickoff it has no Robot yet.
_Avoid_: Current year, upcoming season

**Robot**:
A machine Cranberry Alarm built during a Season.
_Avoid_: Bot, build

**Past Robot**:
A Robot from any Season other than the Featured Season.

**Resource**:
A public link that belongs to a Robot: its code, its video, or its ChiefDelphi thread.
_Avoid_: Link, asset

**Sponsor**:
An organization that gives Cranberry Alarm money or product. An organization that is also a Partner is listed only as a Sponsor.
_Avoid_: Supporter, backer

**Partner**:
An organization Cranberry Alarm works alongside without payment.
_Avoid_: Affiliate, collaborator

**Impact Number**:
A headline count of Cranberry Alarm's reach, stated as of a given date.
_Avoid_: KPI, stat, metric
