We build a competition robot in three days, then give it all away.

Cranberry Alarm is Central Indiana's Robot in 3 Days team. Every January we design, build and program a robot for the new FIRST Robotics Competition game, then publish the code, CAD and video so any team can use them.

About

A small team of FRC alumni and mentors in Greenwood, Indiana.

We have been building since 2023, hosted by FRC 1741 Red Alert Robotics, and between us we have more than 35 years of FRC experience. In the three days after kickoff we aim for a robot that is competitive but simple enough for any team to copy, and we document how we got there.
